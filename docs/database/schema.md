# Database Schema Overview
- `users`: Participant identities, DPDP masked Aadhaar, Discom CA tokens, and OTI metrics.
- `spaces`: Properties, GPS coordinates, geofence radius, room QR tokens, and AI suitability scores.
- `bookings`: Reservation lifecycles, arrival PINs, telemetry timestamps, and micro-lease digests.
- `reviews`: Legacy qualitative reviews.
- `space_inquiries`: Automated AI pre-answers for host-guest inquiries.
- `telemetry_logs`: Immutable audit trails for GPS check-ins, QR scans, and escrow refunds.

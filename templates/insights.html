🌿 Why Your Cycle is the 5th Vital Sign

The American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (ACOG) recognizes the menstrual cycle as an essential vital sign alongside pulse, blood pressure, body temperature, and respiration. Variations in cycle length provide immediate clinical windows into endocrine function, thyroid balance, stress load, and metabolic health.

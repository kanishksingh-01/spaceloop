The Architecture of SpaceLoop

Unlocking Dead Space with Applied AI

In every city, millions of square feet sit empty: garages during work hours, cafes at night, vacant commercial storefronts between multi-year leases. SpaceLoop makes this space liquid, safe, and productive.

1. Multimodal Space Inspection

Instead of asking hosts to fill out complex forms, our vision models extract square footage estimates, ambient natural lighting, acoustic background noise levels, and electrical outlet capacities from photos and videos.

> Auto-detects: 320 sqft • <36 dB acoustic quiet • 4x 20A grounded circuits

2. Natural Language Matchmaking

Say goodbye to rigid dropdown filters. Seekers type their genuine human intent (e.g. "Podcast studio for 2 people" or "Client meeting room near Indiranagar under ₹150/hr"). Our matching engine parses activity requirements, amenities, and budgets to score candidate spaces from 0 to 100%.

> Compatibility Rank: 98% Match for Team Sprint & Client Consultation

3. Instant AI Micro-Leasing

Tenancy laws make property owners afraid to rent temporarily. SpaceLoop solves this by automatically synthesizing an enforceable Temporary Space License Agreement tailored to the exact activity, hours, noise curfew, and clean-up duties with mutual liability waivers.

> Revocable License • No Tenancy Rights • Mutual Indemnity

4. Dynamic Micro-Pricing Engine

Owners rarely know what an empty garage, meeting suite, or studio is worth per hour. Our pricing algorithms calculate optimal hourly and daily rates based on local alternatives, saving seekers up to 65% while creating predictable passive income for hosts.

> ₹15,000/mo avg host earnings • 65% cheaper than commercial coworking

5. Zero-Hardware Telemetry & OTI

No expensive smart locks required. We rely on a ₹5 laminated door QR, GPS geofencing (<50m radar), and AI visual diffs (checking cleanliness and ensuring fans/lights are switched off) to generate an unforgeable Objective Telemetry Index (OTI) that eliminates fake or retaliatory reviews.

> ₹5 QR Door Pass • GPS Radar • Instant ₹100 UPI Escrow Release

6. Sub-30s Dual-Sided Document Verification

DPDP Act 2023 compliant KYC. Seekers verify via DigiLocker / Aadhaar OTP tokenization. Hosts verify property possession via State Discom electricity bill consumer account numbers and bank account matching via NPCI UPI ₹1 penny drop.

> Zero Raw Aadhaar Stored • Discom CA Match • NPCI Penny Drop

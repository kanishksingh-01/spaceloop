5. AI Should Assist the Product, Not Control It
Value-Driven AI Usage
Use AI where it provides genuine value, such as intent extraction, matching, listing understanding, or conversational assistance.
AI Fallback Direction
Follow the existing AI fallback direction:
Groq → Gemini → deterministic rules
Deterministic Core
Critical business logic must remain deterministic and understandable. The application should remain functional when AI services are unavailable.

Priority: Critical
Scope: AI integration, business logic
